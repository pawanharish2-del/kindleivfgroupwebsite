Frequently Addressed Inquiries

Quick answers to standard clinical queries from our global care network.

A single benchmark cycle of In Vitro Fertilization (IVF) usually takes approximately 4 to 6 weeks. This standard block encompasses baseline ovulation monitoring diagnostics, hormone-induced ovarian stimulation, egg harvest retrieval procedures, laboratory fertilization windows, and structured embryo transfers.

Intrauterine Insemination (IUI) is an internal process where processed sperm is introduced directly into the uterine cavity to optimize natural fertilization. In Vitro Fertilization (IVF) is an advanced laboratory tracking process where mature eggs are combined with sperm outside the human body inside specialized embryology incubation modules before implantation.

Medical guidelines routinely recommend seeking specialized diagnostic consultations after 12 solid months of active unprotected trying for partners under 35 years of age, or after 6 months for individuals aged 35 and above.
