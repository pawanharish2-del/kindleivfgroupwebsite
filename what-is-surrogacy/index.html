What is Surrogacy?

A compassionate, legally governed assisted reproductive pathway that helps intended parents build their biological families when pregnancy is medically unviable.

Surrogacy Laws (2021 Act)
Understanding the Basics

Understanding Gestational Surrogacy

Surrogacy is an arrangement in which a woman (the surrogate mother) carries a pregnancy and gives birth to a child on behalf of intended parents who cannot conceive or carry a baby to term themselves.

Under modern clinical protocols at Kindle Womb IVF, only Gestational Surrogacy is practiced. In gestational surrogacy, the surrogate mother carries an embryo created via In Vitro Fertilization (IVF) using the biological eggs and sperm of the intended parents (or an approved donor). Therefore, the surrogate has no genetic relationship to the child.

Gestational Surrogacy

The surrogate has 0% genetic connection. The embryo is formed by intended parents' gametes via IVF/ICSI.

Medical Indications

When is Surrogacy Recommended?

Surrogacy is a vital medical solution when a woman faces insurmountable uterine or systemic health conditions.

Absent or Damaged Uterus

Congenital absence (Mayer-Rokitansky-Küster-Hauser syndrome) or surgical removal of the uterus (hysterectomy) due to fibroids or severe bleeding.

Repeated IVF Failures

Couples who have experienced recurrent implantation failure (RIF) despite creating top-grade blastocyst embryos.

Severe Medical Conditions

Underlying conditions such as severe heart disease, renal failure, or oncology treatment history where pregnancy poses a mortal risk.

Recurrent Miscarriages

History of repeated unexplained pregnancy losses (recurrent miscarriage) where the uterine lining fails to sustain full-term gestation.

Step-by-Step Journey

How Does Surrogacy Work?

The structured clinical and legal pathway at Kindle Womb IVF.

1

Clinical Assessment & Eligibility Verification

Comprehensive evaluation of the intended parents' medical history to verify indication for surrogacy and obtain the required medical indication certificate as per the regulatory board.

2

Ovarian Stimulation & Embryo Creation

The female partner (or egg donor) undergoes gentle hormonal stimulation followed by egg retrieval. Eggs are fertilized with the intended father’s sperm via ICSI to develop healthy Day-5 blastocysts.

3

Surrogate Preparation & Endometrial Priming

The surrogate mother undergoes comprehensive psychological, general medical, and uterine screening. Her endometrium is primed with medication to ensure optimal receptivity.

4

Embryo Transfer Procedure

A selected high-grade blastocyst is gently transferred into the surrogate mother’s uterus under ultrasound guidance. A blood pregnancy test (Beta-hCG) is conducted 14 days later.

5

Antenatal Care & Delivery

Throughout the 9 months of pregnancy, the surrogate receives comprehensive obstetric monitoring, nutritional support, and care at Kindle Womb, culminating in a safe delivery where legal parentage is directly assigned to the intended parents.

Statutory Compliance

Indian Surrogacy (Regulation) Act, 2021 & ART Act Guidelines

In India, surrogacy is governed by the Surrogacy (Regulation) Act, 2021 and the Assisted Reproductive Technology (Regulation) Act, 2021. These laws strictly mandate:

Altruistic Surrogacy Only: Commercial surrogacy is prohibited in India. Surrogates cannot receive commercial compensation beyond medical and insurance expenses.
Essential Eligibility Certificate: Intended couples must obtain a Certificate of Essentiality and Eligibility from the appropriate District/State Medical Board.
Age Criteria for Intended Parents: The female partner must be aged between 23 to 50 years, and the male partner between 26 to 55 years.
Surrogate Mother Criteria: Must be an ever-married woman aged 25 to 35 years with at least one biological child of her own.
