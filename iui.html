Intrauterine Insemination (IUI)

IUI: A Minimally Invasive Step to Parenthood

Intrauterine Insemination (IUI) treatment is a minimally invasive fertility treatment in India by which washed and processed sperm are inseminated into the uterine cavity at the time of ovulation. This IUI procedure significantly increases the chances of pregnancy in infertile couples by placing the highest quality sperm directly near the egg.

IUI is a widely used and highly preferred option at Kindle Womb IVF Fertility Center because it is a minimally invasive, lower-cost alternative to in vitro fertilization (IVF), and it can be conveniently performed in the comfort of our clinic. Our specialized preparation process ensures only the most robust sperm are used for insemination.

When Should One Go for IUI Treatment?

IUI is the most preferred and common form of artificial insemination and is recommended by doctors as an initial form of treatment. A couple may opt for IUI or Intrauterine Insemination in all the cases given below.

  • Antisperm antibodies (Cervical Mucus Problems)
  • Problems in Ovulation
  • Marginal decline in male fertility
  • Unexplained infertility
  • Endometriosis (Mild Cases)
  • Couples using donor sperm

The IUI Treatment Process at Kindle Womb

01.

Ovarian Stimulation

After primary consultation, the female partner takes prescribed fertility medicines. Our expert monitors the development of the follicles in the ovary with the help of an ultrasound. Then, an HCG injection is given to trigger a healthier ovulation process.

02.

Ovulation Tracking

During the stimulation, our fertility expert continuously tracks the female partner's ovulation to determine the precise timing of the IUI. We use monitoring techniques to identify the most fertile window during her menstrual cycle to maximize the chances of success.

03.

Sperm Collection & Washing

A sperm sample is collected from the male partner on insemination day. The sample is meticulously processed, washed, and enhanced in our laboratory. This vital process separates the individual, healthiest sperm from the semen, eliminating non-motile cells.

04.

Intrauterine Insemination

The washed and concentrated sperm are injected directly into the uterus using a small, flexible catheter. This procedure significantly reduces the distance the sperm must travel, bypassing the cervix and increasing the concentration of high-quality sperm.

05.

Post-Procedure Care & Test

Our expert may prescribe specific medicines to increase the chances of pregnancy. Pregnancy tests are conducted within a specific period (typically 14 days later) to check whether the female partner has successfully conceived. We provide dedicated support during this period.
