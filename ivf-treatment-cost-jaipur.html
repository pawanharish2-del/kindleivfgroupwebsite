Breaking Down the Cost of IVF

The total expense of an IVF cycle is rarely a single flat fee. It comprises several moving parts tailored to your specific clinical needs:

  1. Stimulation Medications: The cost of gonadotropin injections required to stimulate egg production varies significantly depending on your AMH levels and body weight.
  2. Clinical and Laboratory Fees: This encompasses the ultrasound monitoring, the egg retrieval surgical procedure, and the highly specialized embryology lab work (fertilization and blastocyst culturing).
  3. Advanced Interventions (If Required): Procedures like ICSI (for male infertility), Laser Assisted Hatching, or Embryo Freezing (Vitrification) add to the baseline cost but can drastically improve success rates for specific diagnoses.
