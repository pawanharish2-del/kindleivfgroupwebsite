Comprehensive Fertility Treatments We Offer

As a full-spectrum fertility hospital, we offer a vast array of reproductive interventions tailored to address both male and female infertility factors.

In Vitro Fertilization (IVF)

IVF is the cornerstone of modern assisted reproductive technology. This highly controlled process involves stimulating the ovaries to produce multiple eggs, retrieving them under mild anesthesia, and fertilizing them with sperm in our advanced laboratory. The resulting embryos are carefully monitored for several days before the healthiest blastocyst is selected for transfer into the uterus. This method is highly effective for conditions such as blocked fallopian tubes, severe endometriosis, or prolonged unexplained infertility.

Intracytoplasmic Sperm Injection (ICSI)

When severe male factor infertility is diagnosed—such as exceptionally low sperm count, poor motility, or high DNA fragmentation—standard IVF may not be sufficient. ICSI treatment involves the precise injection of a single, morphologically sound sperm directly into the cytoplasm of a mature egg. This advanced micro-manipulation technique dramatically increases fertilization rates and provides hope for couples dealing with complex male infertility.

Intrauterine Insemination (IUI)

For younger couples facing mild male factor infertility, cervical mucus issues, or certain cases of unexplained infertility, IUI serves as an excellent, less invasive first-line treatment. Sperm is thoroughly washed and concentrated in the lab before being placed directly into the uterine cavity precisely at the time of ovulation, facilitating a more natural fertilization environment.

A Step-by-Step Guide to Your IVF Journey

Understanding the clinical timeline can significantly reduce anxiety. Here is the structured pathway you can expect when partnering with us.

  1. Comprehensive Diagnostic Assessment: Your journey begins with a deep dive into your medical history. We conduct advanced transvaginal ultrasounds to assess antral follicle count, perform exhaustive hormonal assays (including AMH, FSH, and Estradiol), and conduct detailed semen analysis to map out your precise reproductive profile.
  2. Ovarian Stimulation and Monitoring: Based on your diagnostics, highly customized hormonal medications are administered to stimulate the ovaries to mature multiple follicles. During this 10 to 12-day window, you will visit the clinic frequently for ultrasound and blood monitoring to ensure your safety and optimize egg maturation.
  3. The Egg Retrieval Procedure: Once the follicles reach optimal size, a trigger shot is administered. Precisely 36 hours later, the eggs are retrieved in a minor surgical procedure performed under light sedation. The process takes less than 20 minutes, and patients generally return home the same day.
  4. Fertilization and Embryo Culture: The retrieved eggs are immediately transferred to our Level-3 lab where they are fertilized with sperm using standard IVF or ICSI. Over the next 5 to 6 days, our senior embryologists carefully monitor cellular division within specialized incubators until they reach the robust blastocyst stage.
  5. Preimplantation Genetic Testing (Optional): For patients with a history of recurrent miscarriage or advanced maternal age, we highly recommend PGT-A. This advanced technique screens the embryos for chromosomal abnormalities, ensuring that only genetically competent embryos are selected for transfer, drastically reducing the risk of pregnancy loss.
  6. Embryo Transfer: The culminating step is the embryo transfer. Using real-time ultrasound guidance, a fine catheter is used to place the chosen blastocyst directly into the most receptive area of the uterine lining. This is a painless procedure that requires no anesthesia.

Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

How much does IVF treatment cost in Jaipur?

The total cost of IVF fluctuates based on the complexity of your case, the dosage of stimulation medications required, and whether advanced techniques like ICSI, PGT-A, or donor gametes are utilized. At Kindle Womb, we pride ourselves on transparent, itemized billing. During your initial consultation, we provide a concrete financial estimate and can connect you with zero-interest EMI financing options to ease the financial burden.

What is the success rate of IVF at your center?

While we maintain some of the highest clinical pregnancy rates in Rajasthan—often exceeding global benchmarks for favorable demographics—it is medically unethical to guarantee a pregnancy. Success rates are highly dependent on the female partner's age, ovarian reserve, and the underlying cause of infertility. We utilize advanced technologies like blastocyst culturing and laser-assisted hatching to maximize these odds.

Is the IVF process painful?

The majority of the IVF process involves self-administered subcutaneous injections which cause minimal discomfort. The egg retrieval is performed under light, short-acting anesthesia, so you will feel absolutely no pain during the procedure. Mild cramping post-retrieval is common but easily managed. The embryo transfer feels similar to a routine cervical smear (Pap test) and requires no pain relief.

Can I choose the gender of my baby through IVF?

No. Under the Pre-Conception and Pre-Natal Diagnostic Techniques (PCPNDT) Act of India, sex selection is strictly prohibited and constitutes a severe criminal offense. Our clinic strictly adheres to all national legal and ethical frameworks.

How many embryos do you transfer?

To minimize the significant obstetric and neonatal risks associated with multiple pregnancies (twins or triplets), we strongly advocate for Elective Single Embryo Transfer (eSET), especially when transferring high-quality blastocysts. The decision is made collaboratively between you and our clinical team, adhering to the latest national ART guidelines.
