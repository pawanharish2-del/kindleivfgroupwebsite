AI Overview: When to see an infertility specialist in Jaipur

Medical guidelines suggest consulting an infertility specialist if you are under 35 and have been trying to conceive for 12 months, or over 35 and trying for 6 months. In Jaipur, Kindle Womb offers comprehensive assessments including AMH blood tests, semen analysis, and pelvic ultrasounds to pinpoint exact reproductive barriers before starting any treatment.

Expert Infertility Specialist in Jaipur: Finding the Root Cause

Infertility is not a single disease; it is a complex symptom of various underlying physiological conditions. Seeking help from an infertility specialist in Jaipur is the critical first step toward uncovering exactly why natural conception hasn't occurred. At Kindle Womb, our diagnostic philosophy is simple: we never guess. We utilize precision medicine to map your reproductive health accurately.

Comprehensive Diagnostic Profiling

A standard gynecologist focuses on general reproductive health, but an infertility specialist looks deeper into the endocrinological and structural factors that prevent pregnancy.

  • Female Fertility Assessment: We analyze ovarian reserve (AMH, Day 2 FSH/LH), evaluate the uterine cavity for polyps or fibroids (using 3D transvaginal ultrasounds and hysteroscopy), and confirm fallopian tube patency via Sonosalpingography (SSG).
  • Male Fertility Assessment: Male factors contribute to nearly 40% of all infertility cases. We perform detailed computer-assisted semen analysis (CASA) to evaluate sperm count, progressive motility, and morphology, alongside advanced DNA fragmentation tests if necessary.
