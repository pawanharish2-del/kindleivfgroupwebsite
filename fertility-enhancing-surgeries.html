Fertility Enhancing Surgeries

Our fertility enhancement comprises four distinguished procedures and surgeries that help couples correct underlying complications. We are dedicated to removing barriers and enhancing your chances of a successful pregnancy.

Enquire Now
01

Hysteroscopy Procedure

Hysteroscopy is a diagnostic and corrective surgery done using a hysteroscope—a thin, lighted tube inserted inside the vagina to check the cervix and the interior of the uterus.

This procedure aids in getting a closer look at the uterine environment to diagnose the root cause of issues. It is often suggested when a woman experiences abnormal periods (heavy flow or extended cycle length). If abnormalities are found, samples can be taken immediately for testing.

02

Laparoscopic Myomectomy

Laparoscopy, also known as Minimally Invasive Surgery (MIS), is an operation performed in the abdomen or pelvis using small incisions (usually in the navel region) with the aid of a high-definition camera.

The laparoscope aids in both diagnosis and therapeutic interventions. By using only a few small cuts rather than open surgery, recovery is faster and scarring is minimal, making it a preferred method for fertility enhancement.

03

Ovarian Cystectomy

Fluid-filled sacs on the surface of a woman’s ovary are known as ovarian cysts. While many are harmless and dissolve on their own, persistent cysts can hinder fertility.

In cases where cysts do not resolve or cause complications, we perform a Cystectomy. This careful surgical removal of the cyst preserves the healthy ovarian tissue, ensuring that the ovarian reserve remains intact for future conception.

04

Myomectomy Surgery

Myomectomy is a specialized procedure to remove fibroids (non-cancerous muscular layers) from the uterus. Fibroids can cause severe abdominal cramps, heavy bleeding, and implantation failure.

Unlike a hysterectomy, a myomectomy preserves the uterus, making pregnancy possible. We utilize advanced laparoscopic or robotic myomectomy techniques to ensure precision and maintain uterine integrity.
