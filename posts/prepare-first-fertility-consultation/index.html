How to Prepare for Your First Fertility Consultation at Kindle Womb

Taking the first step to schedule a fertility consultation is an empowering decision. Whether you have been trying to conceive for months or years, the initial visit is focused on understanding, diagnosis, and mapping out a path.

Here is a practical guide on how to prepare for your first fertility evaluation session at Kindle Womb IVF in Jaipur.

1. Gather Your Medical History and Records

If you have undergone any prior testing, diagnostics, or treatments, bring those records with you. This includes past semen analysis reports, blood test results (especially hormone panels like AMH, FSH, and TSH), thyroid reports, and imaging records such as HSG scans or pelvic ultrasounds.

Having these documents available helps our team perform a targeted infertility assessment without repeating baseline tests unnecessarily.

2. Note Down Key Menstrual & Lifestyle Details

Be prepared to discuss details about your menstrual cycle (regularity, duration, and pain levels) and lifestyle factors. Keep track of:

  • The date of your last menstrual period.
  • Any history of pelvic surgeries or chronic medical conditions.
  • Current medications, vitamins, or health supplements you are taking.

3. Attend as a Couple

We highly recommend both partners attend the first consultation. Fertility evaluation involves analyzing both male and female factors. A shared understanding helps both partners align on potential treatment pathways, whether that involves lifestyle changes, IUI treatment, or advanced IVF treatment options.

What Happens During the Consultation?

During this session at our Jaipur IVF Center, you will sit down for a comprehensive talk with our specialist team. We will review your medical history, discuss expectations, answer your questions, and outline diagnostic tests if needed. Our focus is to provide a stress-free, supportive, and informative environment.
