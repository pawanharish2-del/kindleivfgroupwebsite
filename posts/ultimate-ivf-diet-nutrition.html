The Ultimate IVF Diet: What to Eat (and Avoid) for a Successful Cycle

While medical protocols, stimulation medications, and embryology lab technology are the pillars of IVF success, preparing your body nutritionally can play a significant supportive role. A balanced diet improves egg and sperm health, metabolic balance, and uterine receptivity.

Here is a practical guide on what foods to emphasize and what to limit during your cycle at Kindle Womb IVF in Jaipur.

What to Eat During an IVF Cycle

A fertility-boosting diet is rich in nutrients that support cellular health and hormone production. Focus on:

  • Lean Proteins: Essential for egg development. Include beans, lentils, eggs, fish, and lean poultry.
  • Healthy Fats: Omega-3 fatty acids help regulate reproductive hormones and improve blood flow. Eat avocados, walnuts, chia seeds, and extra virgin olive oil.
  • Leafy Greens & Colorful Veggies: Packed with folic acid, iron, and antioxidants that protect developing eggs from oxidative damage. Spinaches, broccoli, and bell peppers are excellent choices.
  • Complex Carbohydrates: Keep blood sugar and insulin levels stable. Choose quinoa, oats, brown rice, and whole grains.

What to Avoid or Limit

To reduce systemic inflammation and support hormonal balance, limit the following:

  • Processed Foods & Trans Fats: High levels of trans fats can impair ovulation.
  • Refined Sugars: Cause rapid spikes in blood insulin, which can impact overall egg quality.
  • Alcohol and Smoking: Highly detrimental to both male and female fertility parameters.
  • Excessive Caffeine: Limit caffeine intake to under 200 mg per day.

Hydration is Key

Drinking plenty of water is essential to maintain optimal cell function and blood flow to the uterus during ovarian stimulation. Aim for 2 to 3 liters of filtered water daily.
