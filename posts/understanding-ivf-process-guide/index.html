Understanding the IVF Process: A Step-by-Step Guide

Deciding to undergo In Vitro Fertilization (IVF) is a powerful and hopeful milestone on your family-building journey. To help ease your path, understanding the step-by-step clinical timeline is essential.

This comprehensive guide breaks down the typical IVF timeline into five distinct phases, helping you prepare physically and emotionally for your treatment at our premier IVF Center in Jaipur.

Step 1: Ovarian Stimulation and Monitoring

In a natural monthly cycle, a woman typically produces a single mature egg. For a successful IVF treatment, fertility specialists prescribe customized hormone medications to encourage the ovaries to develop multiple mature follicles.

This phase usually lasts between 8 to 14 days. During this window, regular visits to Kindle Womb IVF are scheduled for ultrasounds and blood hormone assessments, ensuring follicles mature safely.

Step 2: Egg Retrieval

Once the follicles reach optimal size, a trigger injection is administered to finalise egg maturation. Precisely 34 to 36 hours later, the egg retrieval is performed.

This minor surgical procedure is carried out under light sedation to ensure absolute comfort. An ultrasound-guided needle retrieves eggs from the ovarian follicles in about 15 to 20 minutes.

Step 3: Fertilization in the Embryology Lab

On the day of retrieval, the male partner provides a semen sample. The retrieved eggs are combined with the processed sperm.

For cases dealing with male factor infertility, the embryologist may use Intracytoplasmic Sperm Injection (ICSI), where a single selected healthy sperm is injected directly into a mature egg to maximize fertilization prospects.

Step 4: Embryo Culture and Testing

Fertilized eggs (now embryos) are carefully monitored in advanced incubators. Over the next 3 to 5 days, our embryology team reviews cell cleavage rates.

Advanced screening techniques, such as Preimplantation Genetic Testing (PGT-A), are sometimes performed at this stage to confirm chromosomal health before selection.

Step 5: Embryo Transfer

The final clinical step is the embryo transfer. Using a thin catheter, the selected healthy embryo is placed directly into the uterus under ultrasound guidance. This quick procedure is painless and does not require sedation.

After 10 to 12 days, a blood beta-hCG test is scheduled at our clinic to confirm pregnancy.

Conclusion

The IVF process requires patience and scientific precision. Partnering with a dedicated medical facility like Kindle Womb IVF ensures you receive customized care at every phase.
