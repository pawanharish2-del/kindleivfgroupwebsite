Egg Freezing in Jaipur: Cost, Procedure, and Taking Control of Your Future

Fertility preservation (popularly known as egg freezing or oocyte cryopreservation) empowers women to pause their biological clock, giving them the flexibility to focus on education, career, or personal goals.

If you are considering this proactive step, here is a detailed breakdown of costs, timelines, and vitrification technology at Kindle Womb IVF in Jaipur.

Step 1: Ovarian Stimulation

Similar to the first phase of IVF treatment, you will take hormone stimulation medications for 8 to 12 days to encourage multiple follicles to mature. Regular ultrasounds monitor follicle growth at our Jaipur IVF Center.

Step 2: Egg Harvesting (Retrieval)

Oocytes are retrieved in a quick, 15-minute procedure under light sedation. Our fertility specialists use ultrasound-guided aspiration to harvest mature eggs safely from the follicles.

Step 3: Cryopreservation (Vitrification)

Once harvested, the eggs are immediately evaluated in our embryology lab. High-quality mature eggs are cryopreserved using vitrification—a rapid-freezing technology that prevents ice crystals from forming, ensuring maximum survival rates upon thawing.

Understanding the Cost of Egg Freezing

The total investment includes medication stimulation costs, clinical retrieval procedure fees, and recurring annual storage fees. Kindle Womb IVF practices clear, transparent pricing with flexible payment structures.
