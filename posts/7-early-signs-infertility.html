7 Early Signs of Infertility in Men and Women You Shouldn't Ignore

For many couples, fertility challenges are only discovered after attempting to conceive for a long time. However, paying attention to early warning signals can help you seek diagnostics sooner, saving time and stress.

Here are the 7 key indicators of reproductive health challenges in men and women that warrant a professional evaluation at Kindle Womb IVF in Jaipur.

Key Signals in Women

1. Irregular or Absent Periods

If your cycle fluctuates significantly or stops completely, it is a primary sign of ovulatory issues (such as PCOS or thyroid disorders), which are major contributors to female infertility.

2. Severely Painful or Heavy Periods

Extremely painful cramps, backaches, and heavy bleeding are common indicators of endometriosis or uterine fibroids, both of which can impact fallopian tubes and embryo implantation.

3. Hormonal Fluctuations

Symptoms like unexplained skin changes, reduced libido, facial hair growth, or thinning hair can signal endocrine imbalances affecting your ovaries.

4. Pain During Intercourse

Deep pelvic pain during intimacy can point to underlying issues like pelvic inflammatory disease (PID) or endometriosis, which can cause internal scarring.

Key Signals in Men

5. Changes in Libido or Sexual Function

Erectile dysfunction, difficulties with ejaculation, or a drop in sexual drive can indicate hormone imbalances or physical obstacles.

6. Testicular Discomfort or Swelling

Pain, swelling, or a dull ache in the testicles can be a sign of a varicocele (enlarged veins), which raises testicular temperature and impacts sperm parameters.

Shared Factor

7. Unsuccessful Conception Over Time

If you have been actively trying to conceive for over a year (or six months if the female partner is 35 or older) without success, it is time to schedule a diagnostic evaluation.

Early diagnostic screening allows couples to address root causes with simple lifestyle adjustments, IUI cycles, or customized IVF treatment.
