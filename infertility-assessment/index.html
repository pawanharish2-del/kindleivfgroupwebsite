Comprehensive Infertility Assessment

Start Your Journey with Accurate Diagnosis

A complete infertility evaluation at our IVF Centre in India includes essential tests to identify the root causes of infertility in both partners. At our IVF Clinic, couples undergo targeted investigations to detect issues early.

Male and female partners can have multiple infertility issues, and often more than one factor is involved. The evaluation includes essential blood tests, scans, and analyses commonly used in Infertility Treatment India. In most cases, we identify the underlying reason, but at times the cause remains unknown, termed unexplained infertility. Infertility Assessment of Female

Most infertility specialists will advise tests if you have had failed pregnancies or are facing problems in conceiving. The tests will assist you in determining the underlying issues, which can occur even with regular menstrual cycles and a healthy weight.

Personal Medical History & General Tests

Infertility can be caused by hormonal imbalances or pre-existing diseases. General blood tests help in detecting issues such as hypothyroidism, PCOD, and endometriosis, which are common causes of infertility.

Physical Examination

The thyroid gland, breast health, and pelvic examinations help to find the issues underlying infertility. The gynecological exam is necessary because it focuses on your reproductive health.

Diagnostic Procedures

  • Blood tests for hormones: Evaluate hormone levels that influence ovulation and egg quality.
  • Ultrasound Examination: Enables visualization of the ovary and uterus position and structure to detect issues like PCOS, swollen ovaries, or structural abnormalities.
  • Hysterosalpingography (HSG): An X-ray test to outline the uterus’s internal shape and check if your Fallopian tubes are blocked.
  • Hysteroscopy: Aids in detecting all intrauterine changes that may affect implantation or the baby’s growth.
  • Laparoscopy: A minimally invasive surgical procedure to diagnose reasons like endometriosis, blocked fallopian tubes, scar tissue, or fibroids.

Infertility Assessment of Male

Infertility is a serious condition that can affect both men and women. Causes include low sperm count, poor sperm quality, and pre-existing medical conditions. Various assessments help identify the root cause of failed pregnancies.

Personal Medical History

Doctors will inquire about family history, sexual practices (timing, lubricant use), and medical history, such as precocious puberty, STDs, or urinary tract infections, as these can significantly impact fertility.

Physical Examination

One of the first steps in diagnostics is a physical examination, including a scrotal test and general assessment of body habitus to check for proper androgen-dependent masculinization.

Diagnostic Procedures

  • Semen Analysis: The most important test. Examines sperm count, shape (morphology), movement (motility), and presence of infections. Several tests may be performed for accurate results.
  • Hormone Analysis: Evaluates levels of hormones produced by the pituitary gland, hypothalamus, and testicles, which are vital for sexual development and sperm production.
  • Testicular Biopsy: Used when sperm production is suspected to be normal but a blockage in sperm transport is likely.
  • Specialized Tests for Sperm Function: Rare tests to determine how well sperm survive and penetrate an egg.
  • Genetic Analysis: Examines DNA to determine if gene changes are causing infertility issues.

Essential Terms to Know

AMH (Anti-Mullerian Hormone)

Female Fertility Marker

Produced by cells in ovarian follicles. A high level indicates a large number of follicles/eggs (good ovarian reserve). Levels naturally decrease with age.

FSH (Follicle Stimulating Hormone)

Female & Male Fertility Marker

In women, it's responsible for egg maturation. High FSH levels (tested day 2/3 of cycle) indicate poor ovarian reserves. In men, it promotes healthy sperm production.

AFC (Antral Follicle Count)

Female Fertility Marker

The most accurate indicator of ovarian reserves. Measures the number of antral follicles (containing immature eggs) via vaginal ultrasound. High count suggests significant ovarian reserve.

LH (Luteinizing Hormone)

Male Hormone Marker

Stimulates testosterone production in men. An increase in LH level can indicate that the testicles are not producing enough testosterone.

Testosterone

Male Sex Hormone

Controls masculine characteristics. Unusual decline in levels can lead to infertility and is evaluated as part of a complete hormone profile.

Prolactin

Male Hormone Marker

Produced by the pituitary glands. Increased levels can be linked to erectile dysfunction, fertility issues, and lack of energy in men.
