Advanced Care

Therapies & Solutions

Cutting-edge interventions designed to improve egg quality, embryo selection, and implantation success.

Ovarian Rejuvenation

Stimulates new egg growth in women with premature ovarian failure (early menopause) using PRP, BMDC, or Regenerative OTA therapy.

  • Restores Hormone Levels
  • Oocyte Generation

Laser-Assisted Hatching

Uses a focused laser beam to remove a small section of the embryo's outer wall. This helps the embryo "hatch" and significantly improves implantation.

  • Improves Pregnancy Rates
  • Ideal for IVF Failure

Microfluidics

A non-invasive technology to sort sperm. It mimics natural selection to separate healthy sperm with high DNA integrity without causing damage.

  • Boosts Male Fertility
  • Higher Success Rate
Reproductive Genetics

Genetic Screening (PGT & DNA)

PGT-A (PGS)

Screening embryos for chromosomal abnormalities (aneuploidy) to ensure only healthy embryos are transferred.

PGT-M (PGD)

Diagnosis for specific inherited genetic defects, allowing parents to avoid passing conditions to their children.

DNA Fragmentation

Detects sperm with damaged genetic material—a hidden cause of IVF failure and miscarriage that standard tests miss.

Fertility Acupuncture

A traditional therapy to boost blood circulation to reproductive organs, balance hormones, and relieve stress to enhance fertility naturally.

Blastocyst Culture

Extending embryo culture to Day 5 allows for better selection of the strongest embryos, significantly increasing the chance of pregnancy.
