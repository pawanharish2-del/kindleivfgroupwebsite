Debunking Common Myths About Test Tube Babies

There is still a significant amount of social stigma and misinformation surrounding ART (Assisted Reproductive Technology) in India. Let us clarify the facts based on modern medical science:

  • Myth: Test tube babies are genetically modified or artificial.
    Fact: The fertilization merely occurs in a controlled laboratory environment instead of the fallopian tube. The child is 100% your biological offspring.
  • Myth: The pregnancy is high-risk.
    Fact: Once the embryo successfully implants, the gestation period mirrors a natural pregnancy completely. Routine obstetric care is all that is required.
  • Myth: The procedure is dangerously painful.
    Fact: With modern anesthetics and ultra-fine needles, the egg retrieval process is a minor, painless day-care procedure.

Our Specialized Approach to Embryo Culturing

The success of a test tube baby depends heavily on where the embryos live during their first five days of existence. Our embryology laboratory is designed to mimic the human body.

We utilize triple-gas incubators that precisely control oxygen, carbon dioxide, and nitrogen levels. Furthermore, our continuous monitoring systems allow us to observe cellular division without disturbing the delicate environment. This means we only select the most resilient, genetically sound blastocysts for transfer.

Comprehensive FAQ on Test Tube Procedures

How long does the entire cycle take?

From the start of ovarian stimulation to the pregnancy blood test, a typical cycle takes roughly 4 to 6 weeks. However, if we opt for a frozen embryo transfer (FET), the timeline extends slightly to allow your uterine lining to perfectly sync with the embryo.

What happens to leftover embryos?

If your cycle produces multiple high-quality embryos, we use advanced vitrification (flash-freezing) technology to safely store them. These can be used for future sibling cycles without the need to undergo the stimulation and egg retrieval phases again.
