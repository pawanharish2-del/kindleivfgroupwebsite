Why Surgical Sperm Retrieval?

For men diagnosed with Azoospermia (zero sperm in the ejaculate), surgical sperm retrieval offers a highly reliable opportunity to father biological children. By retrieving sperm directly from the epididymis or testes, our reproductive andrologists combine healthy gametes with ICSI (Intracytoplasmic Sperm Injection) for optimal fertilization.

Overcoming Azoospermia

Effective for both Obstructive (blockage) and Non-Obstructive (production) conditions.

Alternative to Vasectomy Reversal

Avoid complex re-anastomosis surgery with direct, swift sperm harvesting.

Biological Paternity

Enables couples to use the husband's own genetic material rather than donor sperm.

Our Specialized Techniques

Advanced Surgical Retrieval Procedures

Each case of male factor infertility is unique. Our expert andrology surgeons choose the most tailored technique depending on whether the condition is obstructive or non-obstructive.

Minimally Invasive
PESA

Percutaneous Epididymal Sperm Aspiration

A gentle needle aspiration technique performed directly on the epididymis under local anesthesia without skin incisions.

Ideal For: Obstructive Azoospermia
Anesthesia: Local Anesthesia
Recovery: Same day (2-4 hours)
Incision: Zero Incisions / No Stitches
Direct Testicular
TESA / TESE

Testicular Sperm Aspiration / Extraction

Direct needle aspiration (TESA) or tiny micro-biopsy (TESE) from the testicular tissue when epididymal sperm is insufficient.

Ideal For: Failed PESA / Mild NOA
Anesthesia: Local / Sedation
Recovery: 24 to 48 hours
Sperm Yield: High Quality Immature Gametes
Microsurgical
MESA

Microsurgical Epididymal Sperm Aspiration

An open microsurgical approach using an operating microscope to isolate dilated epididymal tubules for maximum sperm harvest.

Ideal For: Congenital Vas Absence (CBAVD)
Anesthesia: General / Regional
Advantage: Yields sperm for multiple ICSI cycles
Freezing: High Cryopreservation Rate
Gold Standard
Micro-TESE

Microsurgical Testicular Sperm Extraction

High-magnification (20x-25x) optical search for active seminiferous tubules, maximizing retrieval while preserving tissue.

Ideal For: Non-Obstructive Azoospermia (NOA)
Magnification: 20x to 25x Zeiss Microscope
Safety: Minimal Tissue Removal
Success: Doubled retrieval vs conventional TESE
