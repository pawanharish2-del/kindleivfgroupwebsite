When is IUI Recommended?

IUI is generally considered the optimal first step for couples who meet specific physiological criteria:

  • Unexplained Infertility: When all diagnostic tests (AMH, tubal patency, and semen analysis) return normal, but natural conception eludes the couple.
  • Mild Male Factor Infertility: If sperm count or motility is slightly below average, the laboratory sperm-washing process concentrates the healthiest, most motile sperm for insertion, bypassing the vaginal and cervical barriers.
  • Ovulatory Disorders: Often paired with ovulation-induction medications (like Letrozole or Clomid) to ensure the timing of insemination perfectly matches the release of the egg.
