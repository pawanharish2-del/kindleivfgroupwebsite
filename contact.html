Common Questions

How do I book a first appointment?
You can book directly through the form above, call our helpline (+91 9119107725), or click the "Call Now" button in the navigation bar. We recommend bringing previous medical reports and semen analysis results for your first visit.
Do you offer online video consultations?
Yes, we offer secure video consultations for patients across Rajasthan and other states who cannot visit the clinic physically. You can request a tele-consultation when booking your slot.
What are the consultation timings?
Our outpatient department (OPD) is open Monday through Saturday from 9:00 AM to 7:00 PM. Sunday appointments are available only by prior special request or emergency fertility procedures.
